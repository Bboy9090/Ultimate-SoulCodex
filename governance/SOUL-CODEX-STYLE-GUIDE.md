# Soul Codex Style Guide

Every reading must follow this format. Every AI prompt must enforce this style. Every output must be consistent.

## Required Format

### 1. Headline
One sentence. Active voice. No hedging.

**Good**:
- "You lead through systems mastery"
- "Your relationships deepen through authenticity"
- "You're entering a completion cycle"

**Bad**:
- "You might be someone who could potentially lead"
- "Your relationships may possibly involve authenticity"
- "It's possible you're in some kind of cycle"

### 2. Summary
2-3 sentences. Context on why this matters.

**Good**:
- "Your Virgo Sun emphasizes precision. Combined with your Life Path 8, you're designed for leadership through systems. This configuration makes you invaluable in roles requiring analysis and authority."

**Bad**:
- "You have Virgo Sun. That's analytical. Also, Life Path 8 is about leadership. So you're analytical at leadership things."

### 3. Meaning
Explain what the calculation/placement actually means. Connect to user experience.

**Good**:
- "This means you see inefficiency before anyone else sees it. You notice when systems fail. You instinctively move to fix them. That's not personality flaw—that's your superpower."

**Bad**:
- "Virgo is analytical. Analysis is good. You're good at it. Very good. So good that... [continues repeating]"

### 4. Action
What should the user actually do with this information?

**Good**:
- "Lean into roles where this precision is valued (engineering, operations, quality assurance). Pair with teams that trust your eye for detail. Watch for perfectionism paralysis—good enough today beats perfect tomorrow."

**Bad**:
- "You should probably think about your career. Maybe you could do something that uses your Virgo energy. Or not. Whatever works for you."

### 5. Evidence
Show the calculation. Show confidence. Make it verifiable.

**Good**:
```
Evidence:
├─ Sun in Virgo (natal chart position: 23° Virgo)
├─ Life Path 9 (birth date numerology: 9/17/1990 → 9+1+7+1+9+9+0 = 36 → 3+6 = 9)
└─ Confidence: 98% (both factors well-established)
```

**Bad**:
```
This is based on like... astrology and numbers and stuff. Trust me.
```

### 6. Confidence
State how certain this analysis is. Be honest about uncertainty.

**Good**:
- "Confidence: 94% (Sun sign is definitive, Life Path calculation is standard numerology)"
- "Confidence: 67% (Time of birth estimated; Rising sign is provisional)"
- "Confidence: 81% (calculation is certain, but manifestation varies by circumstance)"

**Bad**:
- "100% this is true" (never, unless calculations are perfect and inputs are certain)
- "Probably might be true?" (never vague like this)

## Sentence Structure

### Length
- **Headlines**: 5-12 words
- **Summary sentences**: 8-18 words
- **Meaning sentences**: 10-25 words
- **Action items**: 5-20 words per bullet

**Why**: Shorter sentences are clearer. Long sentences hide meaning.

### Avoid These Sentence Patterns

**❌ Hedging**
- "Might be"
- "Could be"
- "Possibly"
- "May or may not"
- "Kind of like"
- "Sort of"

**✅ Replace With**
- "Is" (if certain)
- "Tends to be" (if probabilistic)
- "Can be" (if situational)

**❌ Vague Attribution**
- "Some people say"
- "It's believed that"
- "They say"
- "Research shows" (without citing it)

**✅ Replace With**
- "Astrology suggests"
- "Numerology indicates"
- "Human Design theory proposes"
- "Our analysis shows [based on X calculation]"

**❌ Apologetic Language**
- "We know this might not apply"
- "Sorry if this doesn't resonate"
- "This probably isn't true for you"
- "We're not claiming..."

**✅ Replace With**
- State the fact
- Provide confidence level
- Let user decide

## Vocabulary Standards

### Preferred Terms

| Concept | Preferred | Avoid |
|---------|-----------|-------|
| Emotion | Energy | Vibes, Feels, Stuff |
| Strength | Superpower | Gift, Talent, Bonus |
| Challenge | Pattern to navigate | Flaw, Weakness, Problem |
| Learning | Growth cycle | Issues, Baggage, Damage |
| Situation | Manifestation | Happening, Thing, Whatever |
| Change | Evolution/Shift | Transformation (too vague) |
| Unknown | Provisional/Requires verification | Unclear, Weird, Uncertain |

### Forbidden Phrases

Never use:
- "As a Virgo, you..."  (too reductive; use "Your Virgo Sun suggests...")
- "Trust the universe" (vague mysticism)
- "Everything happens for a reason" (manipulative)
- "You're destined to..." (removes agency)
- "The universe wants you to..." (anthropomorphic)
- "Soul contract" (undefined term)
- "Past life" (unverifiable)
- "You must..." (command, not coaching)

## Confidence Wording

### When Confidence Is High (90%+)
"Your calculation shows..."
"This is definitive because..."
"The math is clear..."

### When Confidence Is Moderate (70-89%)
"This pattern suggests..."
"The data indicates..."
"Your chart shows a likely..."

### When Confidence Is Low (50-69%)
"This is provisional because..."
"Requires verification, but suggests..."
"Early signal pointing toward..."

### When You Can't Calculate It
"We can't know this without [specific data]. Current analysis is based on [what we do know]."

---

## Uncertainty Wording

### For Missing Data
"Your birth time is estimated, so your Rising sign is provisional. This affects house placements."

**NOT**: "We're just guessing about your Rising sign so whatever."

### For Interpretation Variation
"Life Path 8 indicates leadership through systems. How that manifests depends on your choices, environment, and other chart factors."

**NOT**: "Life Path 8 might mean you lead sometimes or maybe you don't. Who knows?"

### For Probabilistic Patterns
"Virgo Sun + Life Path 9 suggests analytical leadership. Individuals with this combination frequently gravitate toward systems roles. Your actual path depends on your choices."

**NOT**: "You'll probably be a systems person but maybe not."

---

## Evidence Wording

### Show the Math
"Birth date: 9/17/1990
Calculation: 9+1+7+1+9+9+0 = 36 → 3+6 = 9
Life Path: 9 (Completion/Reflection)"

### Show the Sources
"Sun in Virgo (natal chart position: 23°)
Mercury also in Virgo (reinforces analytical energy)
This connection is: [show chart]"

### Show the Confidence Basis
"Sun sign: 100% confident (exact position at birth)
Moon sign: 95% confident (natal chart verified)
Rising sign: 78% confident (birth time estimated from parent recall)"

## Archetype Naming

### Naming Convention
[Primary Archetype] + [Secondary Quality]

**Good**:
- "Systems Architect" (clear, specific)
- "Boundary Guardian" (implies both protection and structure)
- "Data Mystic" (combines Virgo precision with intuition)
- "Pattern Recognition Sage" (explains the archetype through behavior)

**Bad**:
- "The Helper" (too generic)
- "Virgo Person" (not an archetype)
- "Mystical Data Thing" (too vague)

### Archetype Format
When presenting an archetype:

**Headline**: [Archetype Name]  
**Core Purpose**: [One sentence on why this archetype exists]  
**Expression**: [How it shows up in behavior]  
**Shadow**: [What happens when this archetype is unbalanced]  
**Growth Path**: [How to integrate this archetype's gifts]

## Card Grammar (Tarot)

### When Referring to a Card
"The Hermit (upright) suggests introspection, while The Hermit (reversed) indicates excessive isolation."

**NOT**: "The Hermit is like... reflection and stuff. Or darkness. Depends."

### Card Meanings Must Be Consistent
Every reference to "The Hermit" means the same thing. If interpretation changes, it's a deliberate evolution, not mood-based variation.

### Show the Card + Meaning
"You drew: The Hermit (introspection)
Meaning in your context: You're in a reflection cycle right now"

Every reading must pass:

- [ ] Headline is active, present tense, no hedging
- [ ] Summary contextualizes why this matters
- [ ] Meaning connects calculation to user experience
- [ ] Action is specific and achievable
- [ ] Evidence shows the math
- [ ] Confidence level is stated
- [ ] No forbidden phrases
- [ ] No hedging language
- [ ] Vocabulary is consistent with table above
- [ ] Archetype naming follows convention
- [ ] Sentence lengths vary (5-25 words)
- [ ] User can verify every fact
