Privacy policy · Updated October 23, 2026

Privacy should be readable before it is agreeable.

Soul Codex separates data kept on your device from information used by server-backed features and gives you controls for both.

1. Information we collect

Information you provide. Birth details used to generate a profile; optional behavioral responses; account information for authenticated experiences; selected contact details when you explicitly use the device contact picker; and purchase, subscription, and entitlement records received from approved payment or app-store providers. Soul Codex does not receive raw card numbers, security codes, or card expiration dates.

Information collected automatically. Session identifiers needed for authenticated or server-backed experiences, plus app/browser platform, operating system, screen size, request logs, and error details needed to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

  • Generate and store profile features you request.
  • Provide compatibility analysis and requested AI-assisted guidance.
  • Perform online placement verification only when you explicitly opt in or invoke a server-backed feature that requires it.
  • Send notifications only when you opt in.
  • Verify paid entitlements and operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service.

3. Storage and security

Local-first profile creation and Connections contact records are stored on your device for offline access. Selecting contacts copies only the entries you choose; Soul Codex does not upload the address book for account matching. Requested server-backed features may store the information necessary to complete those requests in configured production storage.

Payment information is entered through approved payment or app-store surfaces. Soul Codex may retain minimal transaction, entitlement, and redacted verification-audit records for paid-access verification, refunds, revocations, fraud prevention, disputes, and legal obligations.

4. Third-party services

  • OpenStreetMap/Nominatim: may receive place text when you explicitly request place resolution.
  • AI providers: may process profile context and a question needed to generate a requested response.
  • Stripe: may host secure web checkout when web purchasing is enabled.
  • Apple App Store and Google Play: process native subscription purchases when native purchasing is enabled.
  • Infrastructure providers: host the app, database, and encrypted network traffic required for requested server-backed features.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent personal information for marketing. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to perform an action you request, operate or secure the service, or process a payment.

Public Soul Codex cards are optional and user-controlled. Private profiles are not public by default. A public card contains only fields you deliberately selected and excludes private profile identifiers, exact birth details, assessment answers, raw verification evidence, and raw Human Design data.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Create a local-only profile without online verification.
  • Opt in to supported server-backed verification when wanted.
  • Inspect profile information shown in the app.
  • Clear local Soul Codex data from the current device.
  • Request permanent server-account deletion through in-app controls or support when account access is unavailable.
  • Create and revoke optional public-card links.
  • Disable notifications through browser or device settings.

7. Children’s privacy

Soul Codex is intended for people age 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. If we learn that we collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it.

8. Policy changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. Significant changes will be presented through the app or another appropriate notice.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or data requests: privacy@soulcodex.app.
