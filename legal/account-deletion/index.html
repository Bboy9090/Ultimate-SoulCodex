Permanent action

Delete your Soul Codex account and data

This public page explains how to request permanent deletion of a Soul Codex account and associated data.

How to request deletion

  1. If you can access Soul Codex: use the permanent deletion control in the app’s account/settings flow. The in-app flow requires an explicit confirmation before server deletion.
  2. If you cannot access the app or account: email privacy@soulcodex.app from the email address associated with the account and request account deletion.
  3. We may ask you to verify account ownership before processing an email request. Do not send passwords or payment-card information.

What will be removed

  • Soul Codex account and saved profiles.
  • Journal entries and shared links associated with the account.
  • Compatibility contacts and notification subscriptions associated with the account.
  • Product-profile and personalization data associated with the account or session where applicable.
  • Local Soul Codex data when you complete the device cleanup flow.

What may be retained

  • Minimal billing transaction, entitlement-verification, refund/revocation, and related audit records may be retained where needed for financial integrity, fraud prevention, dispute handling, or legal obligations.
  • Operational and security request logs may remain with our hosting provider for up to 7 days before automatic expiration. These logs are not used to restore a deleted Soul Codex account or profile.
  • No scheduled production database backups are currently enabled. Deleted account and profile records are therefore not intentionally retained in scheduled backups.
  • If a specific record must be retained to satisfy a legal obligation, it is kept only for the legally required period and is not used for ordinary product personalization.

Deleting the app, clearing a browser, or signing out is not the same as deleting a server-backed account.
