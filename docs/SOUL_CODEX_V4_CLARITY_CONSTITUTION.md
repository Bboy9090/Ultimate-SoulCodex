# Soul Codex V4 Clarity Constitution

## Mission

Help people understand themselves deeply enough to live intentionally.

Soul Codex is not a horoscope feed, prediction machine, identity vending machine, or replacement for professional care. It is a local-first, evidence-aware self-understanding platform.

## Product rule

Every feature must increase understanding rather than merely increase information.

## Every reading must answer

1. What pattern is operating?
2. What evidence supports it?
3. What might it protect?
4. What might it cost?
5. What constructive gift can it carry?
6. Where can it affect relationships, decisions, work, or identity?
7. What is one grounded action the user can test?

## Required reading structure

Each substantial interpretation should expose:

- observation or supported signal
- explanation
- possible deeper need or protective function
- practical impact
- constructive expression
- overused or costly expression
- grounded action
- reflection prompt
- claim kind
- confidence
- evidence references
- limitations and missing data

## Truth boundaries

Soul Codex must:

- distinguish verified, derived, inferred, self-reported, approximate, and unavailable information
- fail closed when time-sensitive or verification-sensitive data is missing
- never silently promote approximate information to verified fact
- never invent biography, trauma, diagnosis, motives, relationships, or life events
- never treat agreement between related symbolic systems as independent scientific proof
- preserve lived experience as the user's final authority
- invite correction rather than defend a mismatched interpretation

## Tone

The app should be direct, calm, compassionate, and specific.

Prefer:

- "Your verified profile supports..."
- "This may show up when..."
- "One possible protective function is..."
- "The available evidence does not establish why this developed."

Avoid absolute identity claims, flattery, fear-based predictions, dependency language, and mystical certainty.

## Experience hierarchy

The primary destinations are:

1. Identity: Who am I?
2. Reading: Why do I operate this way?
3. Timeline: Where am I now?
4. Compatibility: How do I connect?

The default dashboard should emphasize one insight, one action, and one clear next step rather than a wall of competing cards.

## Visual direction

Use Project Clarity's information hierarchy with restrained Nebula atmosphere:

- midnight violet-black surfaces
- antique gold accents
- subtle blue and violet depth
- serif display typography with readable sans-serif body text
- large touch targets
- restrained motion
- clear contrast
- no cartoon astrology or crystal-ball clichés
- no unsupported ratings, user counts, medical claims, or authority claims

## Success test

A successful session leaves the user thinking:

> I understand why I do that, what it costs, and what I can try next.

Not merely:

> I learned another label.
