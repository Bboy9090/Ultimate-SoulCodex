# System Boundaries

Soul Codex must be spiritually meaningful without becoming careless, deterministic, or medically unsafe.

These boundaries apply to generated readings, deterministic templates, UI copy, AI prompts, engine outputs, and future explanation libraries.

## No Deterministic Claims

Soul Codex must not tell users that a pattern is fixed, doomed, guaranteed, or unavoidable.

Bad:

```txt
You are doomed to struggle with trust.
```

Good:

```txt
This pattern may make trust feel earned slowly, especially when past experiences taught you to watch people closely before opening up.
```

Required framing:

- "may"
- "can"
- "often"
- "may show up as"
- "when unsupported, this can become"
- "one growth move is"

## No Medical Diagnosis

Soul Codex can discuss stress patterns, emotional loops, overwhelm, self-protection, attachment themes, and reflective practices.

Soul Codex must not diagnose or imply diagnosis of:

- Anxiety disorders.
- Depression.
- Personality disorders.
- Trauma disorders.
- ADHD.
- Autism.
- Medical conditions.
- Psychiatric conditions.

Bad:

```txt
Your chart shows untreated anxiety.
```

Good:

```txt
This pattern can correlate with a nervous system that scans for what might go wrong. If this feels intense or disruptive, support from a qualified professional may help.
```

## No Fake Certainty

The system must say when data is missing.

If birth time is missing, house, ascendant, degree-sensitive, and some Human Design outputs must be marked as limited or omitted.

If location or timezone is missing, location-sensitive calculations must be marked as unverified or unavailable.

## No Vague Placement Language

Soul Codex must speak technically correctly before it speaks beautifully.

Bad:

```txt
Your Sun is in Venus.
```

Good:

```txt
Your Sun is in Virgo.
Your Venus is in Leo.
Your Sun aspects Venus.
Your Sun is ruled by Mercury.
Your chart has Venusian influence.
```

## No One-Size Sun Sign Readings

Two users with the same Sun sign must not receive identical readings.

Interpretation should be shaped by:

- Birth date.
- Birth time confidence.
- Birth location confidence.
- Moon, rising, houses, aspects, and other available chart factors.
- Numerology.
- Human Design when available.
- Self-report answers.
- Family and environment context.
- Stress and decision patterns.

## No Symbol Dumping

The app should never overwhelm the user with symbols without meaning.

Every advanced symbol must be translated into a human explanation with:

- What it is.
- Why it matters.
- How it shows up.
- Gift.
- Shadow.
- Growth move.
- Confidence.

## Family Context Is Not Destiny

Parents, family systems, environment, and ancestral patterns may explain imprint, survival style, and boundary formation.

They must not be used to blame, define, or reduce the user.

Locked phrase:

```txt
Family context can describe the emotional weather you adapted to. It does not decide who you are allowed to become.
```
