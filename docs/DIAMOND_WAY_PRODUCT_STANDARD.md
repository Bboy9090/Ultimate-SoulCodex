# Soul Codex — Diamond Way Product Standard

## North Star

Soul Codex should feel less like a catalog of astrology/numerology labels and more like a high-trust co-pilot for self-understanding.

The product promise is:

> Reveal the pattern. Show the tension. Name what is known. Invite the user to test the meaning in lived experience.

The reading must never become more certain merely because more symbolic systems are available.

## The Diamond Way

Every meaningful reading follows six moves.

1. **Signal** — state the actual calculated or user-supplied evidence.
2. **Resonance** — explain what multiple supported signals reinforce.
3. **Tension** — surface supported contradictions instead of flattening them into one personality adjective.
4. **Mirror** — turn the synthesis into a question the user can confirm, reject, or refine.
5. **Choice** — offer one concrete action, boundary, experiment, or conversation.
6. **Receipt** — keep provenance, confidence, missing data, and unsupported systems visible.

This is the default quality bar for Identity, Compatibility, Timeline, Daily Pulse, Soul Guide, and future AI-generated interpretation.

## Identity Integrity

No profile may silently inherit another profile's language.

Forbidden behavior:

- unsupported numerology falling back to Life Path 9, 1, 6, or any other valid archetype;
- unsupported zodiac labels falling back to Virgo or another valid sign;
- missing birth time becoming a fabricated noon chart;
- missing Moon, Rising, houses, aspects, nodes, Chiron, or Human Design being converted into guessed placements;
- stale saved archetype prose surviving after its underlying evidence becomes invalid;
- contact-only people being presented as complete birth charts.

Unsupported values may remain visible as raw data, but they must not influence interpretive prose until governed.

## Material Differentiation

Passing differentiation means more than inserting a person's name into the same template.

A profile should materially change when any verified high-weight signal changes:

- Big Three;
- Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Pluto;
- house placement and angular geometry;
- strongest verified aspects;
- elemental and house concentrations;
- verified Nodes/Chiron;
- governed numerology;
- independently verified Human Design core;
- user-confirmed lived-experience signals.

Quality gates should measure exact duplication, repeated layer summaries, and phrase overlap across a large corpus.

## Sacred / Galactic Lens

Soul Codex may offer an optional spiritual frame using language such as **sacred pattern**, **galactic code**, **divine blueprint**, **Federation lens**, or **God-given potential** when the user chooses that mode.

That mode is a meaning-making lens, not an empirical claim.

The UI must keep three things separate:

- astronomical or deterministic calculation;
- symbolic interpretation;
- spiritual belief or personal meaning.

A spiritual frame may deepen reflection, but it may not manufacture evidence or increase factual confidence.

## The Co-Star Difference

The goal is not to copy Co-Star's voice.

Soul Codex should distinguish itself through:

- cross-system synthesis instead of isolated placements;
- contradiction-aware readings instead of one-note traits;
- explicit evidence and uncertainty;
- relationship and decision context;
- lived-experience feedback loops;
- a progression from insight to action;
- personal language that changes because the underlying pattern changed.

## Reading Voice

Preferred voice: precise, intimate, sharp, slightly cinematic, never generic.

Avoid filler such as:

- “you are intuitive” without a source;
- “trust the universe” as a substitute for analysis;
- repeated “discernment,” “alignment,” or “purpose” language when the actual profile does not support it;
- one adjective presented as destiny.

Prefer language that names interaction:

> Your verified Moon signal asks for X while the strongest Mars pattern pushes Y. The useful question is not which one is “the real you,” but what happens when both are active under pressure.

## Diamond UI Direction

Visual identity: cosmic noir with refractive diamond energy.

Use:

- deep dark-space surfaces;
- luminous glass and prism refractions;
- restrained star-field motion;
- sharp typographic hierarchy;
- high-contrast evidence chips;
- a signature diamond/facet motif for synthesis;
- richer motion only when it communicates state, depth, or transition.

Avoid visual noise that makes the app look like a horoscope feed.

The premium moment is the **Diamond Synthesis**: a faceted visual showing the strongest signals, resonances, tensions, and unresolved layers around the user.

## Core Surfaces

### Identity
Lead with the user's unique synthesis, not a grid of systems.

### Daily Pulse
Connect today's reflection to stable birth-chart/numerology signals and recent lived entries. Do not treat transits or symbolic timing as deterministic prediction.

### Compatibility
Explain interaction between two distinct patterns. Avoid generic “good/bad match” scoring as the primary answer.

### Timeline
Show what changed in the user's own logged experience and which symbolic patterns seem relevant. Clearly distinguish observed history from symbolic timing.

### Soul Guide
Operate as an interactive reflection partner. It should cite the user's active evidence model, ask high-information questions, and remember user-confirmed refinements without overriding source truth.

## Federation Work Lanes

The Federation is the engineering/product QA model, not a source of mystical authority.

Parallel lanes:

- Evidence Integrity
- Astrology Verification
- Numerology Governance
- Human Design Governance
- Cross-System Synthesis
- Language Differentiation
- Lived-Experience Learning
- Compatibility
- Timeline
- Visual / Motion Design
- Accessibility
- Release / Store Qualification

Each lane produces tests or receipts. No lane can declare a symbolic claim “true” merely because another lane generated similar prose.

## Release Gates

A Diamond Way release should not ship unless:

- hidden identity fallbacks are absent;
- invalid or missing evidence fails closed;
- a differentiation corpus has zero exact duplicate narratives;
- materially different profiles produce materially different synthesis;
- unsupported systems remain visibly unresolved;
- user-confirmed lived data never rewrites astronomical source truth;
- the production UI consumes the same qualified synthesis that passed tests;
- release receipts identify the exact SHA used for the build.
